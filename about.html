About Hardeep

Local leadership with calm energy and real follow-up.

Hardeep is running to bring calm, organized, resident-first service to Ward 4 — with greener neighbourhoods, safer streets, and better follow-up at Town Hall.

His Story

Why Hardeep is running.

Hardeep Ghuman is running for City Councillor in Ward 4 because residents deserve a councillor who is approachable, organized, and focused on practical results.

His campaign is focused on practical local problem-solving: hearing residents clearly, routing concerns properly, and reporting back with timelines and outcomes.

The tone is intentionally gentle: greenery, service, trust, and community — without heavy political language.

  • Accessible to residents before and after election day.
  • Focused on basics: roads, parks, safety, service, taxes, and growth.
  • Respectful toward residents, Town staff, volunteers, and local businesses.
