Ward 4 Voting

Vote online or in person.

Choose the option that works best for you. Online advance voting runs October 9–24, and in-person voting takes place on Election Day, October 26.

💻
Online Advance Voting

October 9–24, 2026

Vote online from Friday, October 9 at 9:00 a.m. until Saturday, October 24 at 11:59 p.m.

Online voters must opt in first. Registration is available from September 1 at 9:00 a.m. until October 24 at 6:00 p.m.

Election Day

Monday, October 26, 2026

In-person voting is scheduled from 10:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Ward 4 voters can vote at one of the locations below.

Gellert Community Centre10241 Eighth Line, Georgetown
Holy Cross Parish14400 Argyll Road, Georgetown
