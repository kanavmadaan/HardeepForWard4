# Website Photo Checklist

This version does **not** use generated placeholder visuals for the content photos. Add your own real JPG photos into:

`assets/images/`

Use these exact lowercase file names so the website loads correctly on phones and hosting platforms:

1. `1.jpg` — Hardeep Ghuman portrait / candidate headshot
2. `2.jpg` — Halton Hills green space, park, trail, trees, or rural landscape
3. `3.jpg` — Georgetown / Halton Hills main street or local business street photo
4. `4.jpg` — Ward 4 neighbourhood photo, residents walking, street, park, or community scene
5. `5.jpg` — Hardeep with residents, local community conversation, or approachable candid photo
6. `6.jpg` — Town Hall, municipal building, public service desk, or civic operations photo
7. `7.jpg` — Halton Hills heritage, rural road, farm, historic building, or countryside photo
8. `8.jpg` — Heritage street, historic home/building, or community landmark photo
9. `9.jpg` — Georgetown South / Ward 4 street, neighbourhood, or local plaza photo
10. `10.jpg` — Campaign volunteers, sign team, community meeting, or contact/get-involved photo

## Ward map

The Ward 4 map is not part of the 10-photo checklist. It is included separately as:

- `assets/images/ward-4-georgetown-south-map.png`
- `docs/Ward-4-Map.pdf`

Source: Town of Halton Hills Ward 4 Map PDF provided for this website build.

## Important

Keep the file extension as `.jpg`, not `.jpeg`, `.JPG`, or `.png`, unless you also update the HTML image paths.
